Interactive Geometric Portal - Concept Summary
Core Structure:
A nested geometric composition centered on screen, consisting of three layers:

Center: A diamond with dynamic symbols
Middle: A golden pulsing circle (radius = 2× diamond diagonal) connected to the diamond by 4 black lines with animated sparks
Outer: A screen-filling square connected to the circle by 4 corner lines

Interactive Behavior:
Hover State:

Text rises from the hovered line (emerging from horizon)
Diamond symbol transforms to represent that line's theme
Corresponding text orbits continuously around the golden circle

Click State (Sequential animation):

Current animations freeze and complete
Entire center structure (diamond + circle + 4 lines) rotates to align with outer square's diagonal
Outer square splits into 4 triangular segments that pull away from center, revealing content beneath

Thematic Design:
Four cardinal lines represent distinct concepts (Knowledge, Power, Wisdom, Harmony), each with unique:

Rising text label
Diamond symbol glyph
Orbital circle inscription

Visual Aesthetic:
Futuristic interface with glowing effects, gradient fills, pulsing animations, and spark particles - suggesting a portal or gateway that responds to user exploration and ultimately opens to reveal deeper content.
